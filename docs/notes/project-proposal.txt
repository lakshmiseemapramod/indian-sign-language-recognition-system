Aim of the project:To develop an instrument that interprets sign language into spoken English 
Significance:It can be challenging to communicate with a disabled person who is deaf or mute, particularly if you don't know sign language.We are creating a device to solve this issue in 
              order to facilitate communication.

disadvantage:
Uses motion capture sensors that consists of 17 sensors ,full body suit and a strap set with battery life of 6 hrs with range of 25m. This is only for motion capture and requires
other equipments for storing the database containing the language materials.Highly qualitative and accurate in representing a sign (or action) as spatio temporal skeletal joint information
but is very costly.
